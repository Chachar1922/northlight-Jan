Frequently asked questions

How much does a design project cost?

Most brand identity projects start from 8,000 pounds and most website projects from 15,000 pounds. We send a fixed quote after a short discovery call, so there are no surprises.

How long does a project take?

A brand identity usually takes six to eight weeks. A website or product design takes eight to twelve weeks, depending on size and how quickly feedback arrives.

Do you work with clients remotely?

Yes. Our 25-person team works with clients in person at our studio and remotely through video calls and shared design files.

How do I start a project with Northlight Studio?

Send us a short enquiry using the contact form on this page. A member of our sales team replies within one working day to arrange a discovery call. Urgent enquiries are prioritised.
